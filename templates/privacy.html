Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the service, we also store the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate the core features of Spendly, such as displaying your expense history, generating category breakdowns, and summarizing your spending over time. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are accessible only to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is ever completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal expense data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers to host the application and store data on our behalf; these providers are bound to handle data only as instructed by Spendly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
